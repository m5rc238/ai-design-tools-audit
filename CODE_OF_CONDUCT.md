# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, constructive environment for all contributors.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional
- Focus feedback on evidence and arguments
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions
- Accept and give constructive critique

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or intimidation
- Hate speech or discriminatory conduct
- Deliberate misrepresentation of evidence

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for behavior that violates this code.
