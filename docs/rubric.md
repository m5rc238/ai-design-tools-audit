# Scoring Rubric (v0.1)

Scores are 1-5 where:

- 1 = weak or absent signal
- 3 = mixed or moderate signal
- 5 = strong and repeatedly evidenced signal

## Dimensions

### 1) Transparency

- 1: Marketing-level AI claims only
- 3: Partial implementation disclosure
- 5: Clear architecture/provider/limits disclosure

### 2) Workflow Depth

- 1: Isolated AI feature
- 3: Multi-step workflow support
- 5: End-to-end workflow with persistent context

### 3) Ecosystem Embedding

- 1: Minimal integrations
- 3: Standard integrations in common stacks
- 5: Deep integrations that materially change team operations

### 4) Differentiation Evidence

- 1: Commodity prompt or generation pattern
- 3: Distinct packaging/orchestration but no hard moat evidence
- 5: Strong evidence of compounding advantage or proprietary assets

### 5) Switching Cost Signals

- 1: Easy replacement with low migration cost
- 3: Some operational friction to switch
- 5: High switching friction due to embedded workflows, history, or collaboration artifacts
